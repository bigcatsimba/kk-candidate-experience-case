Candidate Application Journey — A Small Process Improvement Case

An observation made while completing an application, translated into a practical process and UX improvement proposal.

Independent candidate case study · not an official KK Group page

Peipei Jiang · Barcelona · Application for Senior Specialist – AI & Excellence · simbajiang30@gmail.com

1. What I observed

The objective is not to criticise the interface, but to identify friction that can affect completion, recovery and candidate experience.

Impact is judged by three questions: does it make an error hard to reverse, does it reduce the quality of the data received, and how much effort would the fix need?

01 · FUNCTIONAL / RECOVERY

CV recovery

After uploading a CV, the application flow did not provide a reliable way to replace it when the uploaded version needed to be corrected.

High impact
02 · DATA / FORM DESIGN

Multilingual information

The language section appeared to support one language entry, without an obvious way to add additional languages. This is a mismatch with naturally multi-value candidate data.

Medium impact
03 · USABILITY / HIERARCHY

Selection and attachment clarity

The scroll-based multi-select for country and nationality takes more effort than a searchable selector would. On the same page, the state of each attachment and the next required action could be clearer.

Lower impact

2. How I would approach it

A simple process-excellence lens before jumping to a solution.

01
Identify frictionWhere does the candidate hesitate, fail or need to recover?
02
Diagnose causeIs it a functional defect, data-model limitation or usability issue?
03
Prioritise impactWhich friction affects completion, quality or candidate experience most?
04
Redesign & measureChange the workflow, then validate the improvement with evidence.

4. How I would measure the improvement

The redesign should be validated through outcomes, not aesthetics.

Completion rateFewer candidates abandoning the application.
Recovery successAbility to replace or correct an attachment without restarting.
Time to completeLower effort for common form interactions.
Candidate feedbackImproved clarity and confidence in the submission process.

Operational improvement, capability building and hands-on AI workflow design.

AI invoice translation workflow

Process improvement is the point.

The objective is not to optimise the interface for its own sake, but to remove friction from the process and make the candidate journey easier to complete, recover and understand.
